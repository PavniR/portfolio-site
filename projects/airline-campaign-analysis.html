Case Study

Airline Loyalty Program & Campaign Analytics

Turning customer and flight activity data into actionable insights on loyalty performance, campaign effectiveness, and customer retention through SQL, Python, and interactive Power BI dashboards.

Dataset16,700+ airline loyalty members with customer profiles, flight activity, and campaign history
TechnologiesPython, SQL, Power BI, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn
GitHub Repository

Business Problem

The airline knew who its members were, but not what was driving loyalty or campaign success.

Leadership lacked a unified view of customer loyalty performance, campaign effectiveness, and member engagement. Marketing campaigns generated enrollments, but it was unclear whether they attracted valuable customers, improved retention, or strengthened the loyalty program. The objective was to transform raw customer and flight data into actionable insights for marketing and customer retention teams.

Objectives

What the analysis needed to answer.

  • Measure the overall health and growth of the loyalty program.
  • Evaluate the effectiveness of the 3-months long 2018 promotional campaign.
  • Identify the characteristics of high-value loyalty members.
  • Analyze customer engagement through flight activity and reward redemption.
  • Deliver interactive dashboards for executive decision-making.

Dataset Overview

What went into the analysis.

The project combines customer demographics, loyalty program information, campaign participation, and monthly flight activity for more than 16,700 loyalty members. The dataset includes customer lifetime value (CLV), salary, loyalty tier, enrollment history, flight distance, reward points, redemption behavior, and geographic information, enabling both customer and campaign analysis.

Combined customer demographics, loyalty program information, campaign participation, and monthly flight activity into a single analytical dataset. This consolidated view enabled customer-level analysis across acquisition, engagement, retention, and loyalty value.

Customer and Campaign Analysis

Finding what drives loyalty and campaign performance.

Explored enrollment trends, loyalty tier performance, campaign effectiveness, customer lifetime value, reward redemption, and geographic distribution to uncover the factors influencing member acquisition, retention, and long-term value.

Executive Dashboard

Turning analysis into decision-ready reporting.

Developed a 2-page interactive Power BI dashboard with custom DAX measures, executive KPIs, campaign performance tracking, customer segmentation, and geographic analysis, allowing stakeholders to explore the loyalty program through dynamic filtering and drill-down interactions.

Key Insights

What the analysis revealed.

  • The 2018 promotional campaign produced a substantial increase in new member enrollments.
  • Aurora members consistently generated the highest average Customer Lifetime Value.
  • Campaign-acquired members experienced lower cancellation rates than non-campaign members.
  • Member concentration remained strongest across Ontario, British Columbia, and Quebec.
  • Reward redemption patterns highlighted opportunities to strengthen long-term customer engagement.

Business Recommendations

Where the airline should focus next.

  • Continue targeted acquisition campaigns modeled after the successful 2018 promotion.
  • Prioritize retention strategies for high-value customer segments.
  • Increase reward redemption incentives to improve ongoing engagement.
  • Invest marketing resources in provinces with the strongest customer concentration.
  • Monitor loyalty KPIs continuously through interactive dashboards.

Future Improvements

Taking the project further.

Future iterations could incorporate customer churn prediction, customer lifetime value forecasting, automated data refresh pipelines, and real-time campaign monitoring to support proactive marketing decisions.

Key Takeaways

The short version.

What business question did this solve?

Understand how the loyalty program was performing, whether the 2018 campaign delivered lasting value, and which customer segments contributed most to long-term member growth and retention.

What technical skills did I strengthen?

Customer-level data integration, SQL analytics, DAX measure development, Power BI data modeling, KPI design, and translating analytical findings into executive-ready dashboards.

If I rebuilt this today, what would I improve?

I'd incorporate predictive churn and customer lifetime value forecasting, along with automated dashboard refreshes and real-time campaign monitoring to support proactive decision-making.
