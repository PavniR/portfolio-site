Case Study

E-Commerce Product Growth Analytics

An end-to-end product analytics case study built on the RetailRocket clickstream dataset. By analyzing the complete customer journey: from product discovery to purchase, the project uncovers where users drop off, identifies high-value customer segments, and delivers recommendations to improve conversion, retention, and long-term customer value.

DatasetRetailRocket E-Commerce Clickstream Dataset, Over 2.7M+ events
TechnologiesPython, PostgreSQL, Power BI, Pandas, Plotly, Matplotlib, Seaborn
GitHub Repository

Business Problem

High website traffic doesn't necessarily translate into revenue.

Despite recording millions of customer interactions, only a small fraction of visitors completed a purchase. Without understanding where customers abandoned their journey or which users generated the greatest long-term value, it was difficult to prioritize product improvements and growth initiatives.

Objectives

What the analysis needed to answer.

  • How efficiently does the platform convert visitors into paying customers?
  • At which stage of the purchase journey do users abandon the process?
  • Which customer groups contribute the highest long-term value?
  • How engaged are users after their first purchase?
  • Which product and retention strategies could improve overall conversion?

Dashboard Showcase

What product managers use weekly.

Executive Performance Dashboard: Provides a high-level view of platform health through KPIs such as user activity, engagement, event distribution, and purchase conversion.

Purchase Funnel: Visualizes customer progression from product view to add-to-cart and finally purchase while measuring conversion at each stage. Pinpoints where users abandon the buying journey, helping product teams prioritize improvements with the greatest potential impact.

RF Customer Segmentation:Groups purchasing users into six behavioral segments using Recency and Frequency scoring. Supports personalized retention strategies, loyalty programs, lifecycle marketing, and CRM targeting.

Dataset Overview

Understanding customer behavior at scale.

The project analyzed approximately 2.76 million clickstream events generated by 1.41 million users across more than 235,000 products. After cleaning and feature engineering, the dataset supported funnel analysis, behavioral segmentation, executive KPI reporting, and customer growth analysis.

Although the original dataset includes product property and category tables, the project focuses on behavioral analytics rather than catalog analysis, making the clickstream events sufficient for answering the business questions.

Business Recommendations

Key Insights

What the data actually said.

  • Most visitors never reached meaningful purchase intent.
    While the platform generated 2.76 million interactions across 1.41 million users, transactions represented only 0.84% of all events, indicating strong acquisition but weak purchase conversion
  • The largest conversion bottleneck occurred before users added products to their cart.
    Only 2.69% of visitors progressed from viewing a product to adding it to their cart. Once customers demonstrated purchase intent, checkout performance improved considerably with a 31.07% Cart-to-Purchase conversion rate.
  • Repeat purchasing remained relatively limited.RF segmentation showed that most buyers completed only a single purchase, with an average purchase frequency of 1.92, highlighting significant opportunities to improve customer retention.
  • Customer behavior varied significantly across lifecycle stages.
    Segmenting users into Champions, High-Value, Emerging, Casual, Declining, and Inactive groups created a clearer picture of customer value beyond simple purchase counts, enabling more targeted lifecycle strategies.

Business Recommendations

What the product team should do differently.

  • Improve product discovery: Increase View-to-Cart conversion through better search functionality, personalized recommendations, and improved merchandising.
  • Optimize product detail page: sStrengthen purchase intent with richer product information, customer reviews, higher-quality imagery, and clearer calls-to-action.
  • Invest in retention for valuable customers: Develop loyalty initiatives targeting Champions and High-Value Users' tiers to maximize long-term customer lifetime value.
  • Re-engage dormant users: Launch personalized campaigns for Emerging, Casual, Declining, and Inactive customers to encourage repeat purchases.
  • Personalize lifecycle marketing: Use behavioral segmentation to tailor CRM communication based on customer engagement patterns instead of treating all users equally.

Challenges Faced

Working with behavioral data & Preparing large scale click-stream data

The dataset did not include transaction values, making traditional RFM analysis not feasible. To overcome this limitation, customer segmentation was redesigned around Recency and Frequency, allowing meaningful behavioral insights without requiring monetary values.
Millions of interaction records required careful preprocessing, feature engineering, and optimized SQL workflows to support efficient analysis and dashboard reporting.

Future Improvements

How this would evolve in production.

In a production setting, this project could be enhanced with automated ETL pipelines, scheduled dashboard refreshes, data quality monitoring, cloud-based storage, and richer event tracking to support continuous, scalable product analytics.

Key Takeaways

quick overview.

Business Value

Identified the largest friction point in the customer journey, segmented users by behavioral value, and translated clickstream data into practical recommendations for improving conversion and retention.

Technical Growth

Demonstrated an end-to-end analytics workflow spanning data preparation, feature engineering, exploratory analysis, SQL-based KPI development, behavioral segmentation, and executive dashboard design using Python, PostgreSQL, and Power BI.

Next Iteration

Future versions could incorporate transaction values for full RFM analysis, event-level attribution, cohort retention analysis, A/B experiment evaluation, and predictive models for conversion and customer churn.
